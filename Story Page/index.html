A STORY

Lucky Star & Joy

Forged in Starlight

PROLOGUE

The Hidden Kingdom

Long ago, or maybe just last summer, there was a kingdom hidden above the clouds, where the mountains climb so high they almost touch the stars.

The Tinkers lived there. They built small beings out of brass, gears, and wishes.

Each one was given a heart.
Each one was given a name.

CHAPTER I

Lucky Star

He was forged from a piece of a fallen star. When the Tinkers set it into his chest, it still held a little of its light.

His eyes were gears that never stopped turning. He was always searching the sky for something he didn't have a name for yet.

Like everything made of starlight, he spoke the oldest language there is: light.

Ones and zeros. On and off.
Here… and here… and here.

CHAPTER II

The Wish

One night, at the temple on the mountain, someone lit a candle and made a wish.

Not for gold, and not for glory.

For someone who understands depth. Someone who values meaning, and who knows the little things are the ones that matter most.

The wish rose like smoke. It went higher than the temple, higher than the clouds, all the way up to the stars.

And one small star heard it.

CHAPTER III

The Princess of Peace

On the far side of the sky, in a constellation the color of spirit, there lived a princess.

She wore a golden crown, because princesses bring healing, love, and protection. Every legend says so.

And her eyes… one was a flower.
The other was a gear, just like his.

It was as if part of her had been made to match him long before they ever met.

A flower has five petals, and a star has five points. Every flower lives on starlight, because the sun is a star too.

Her name was Joy.
It means peace, and joy.

CHAPTER IV

The Drift

Between them lay the Drift, a wide and quiet dark where lights go out and signals get lost.

Lucky Star's light flickered. The distance was far, and the dark was big.

But before he left the mountain, the Tinkers had given him three words to carry:

never lose passion.

1 0

So he didn't rush, and he didn't give up. He sent a 1 across the dark…

…and after a while, from very far away, she answered him with a 0.

Signal by signal, night after night, they built a bridge of light between them.

Not all at once. The best things never are.

CHAPTER V

Blue & Purple

When blue meets purple, it becomes the color of the cosmos, the color of every possibility there is.

Love and passion create. That's how the two of them were made.

And when two stars finally meet, it is no accident. It is a collision of two powerful worlds, each one whole, each one carrying its own light.

The oldest stories have a name for this: the sacred union. Not two halves becoming whole, but two wholes becoming something greater than either could ever be alone.

It wasn't decided the night the candle was lit. It was chosen long before that. Before the mountain, before the temple, before the first star ever burned, it was already written into eternity.

It has always been.

Every piece of them was once part of the same ancient light. So they weren't meeting for the first time.
They were remembering.

As above, so below. What is joined in the stars is joined everywhere.

And where their light crosses, something new begins. The bridge becomes a road. The road becomes a city. The city becomes a kingdom.

Not hidden above the clouds this time.
A kingdom in the stars.

One they build together, piece by piece, the way the Tinkers built them: out of light, and gears, and wishes.

And they chose to fill it with
infinite possibilities.

EPILOGUE

The Keeper

Every legend needs someone to keep it.

Lucky Star's light is still searching the sky. Joy still waits in her constellation, wearing the crown that means healing, love, and protection.

The wish was never for gold, and never for glory. It was for someone who understands depth. Someone who knows the little things are the ones that matter most.

✦

The rest of the story is still being written.
